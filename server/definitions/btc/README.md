# BTC definitions

Machine-readable contracts for BTC implementation work.

- [`sources`](sources) contains fifty V1 source-definition files — forty-nine normalized-stream contracts plus one repair-row contract, after ruling E62 (2026-09-04) deleted the three unreachable Binance USD-M `market`-socket sources — across Coinbase Exchange, Kraken Spot, Binance, Bybit, Chainlink, Pyth Network, Polymarket, and Kalshi; see [`sources/README.md`](sources/README.md) for the per-venue breakdown. Binance and Bybit are rights-gated behind `SIGNALS_ENABLED_VENUES` (ruling R7).
- [`signals`](signals) contains one V1 definition for each of the 193 defined signal identities (190 published; `btc.multi.oracle_dispersion`, `btc.pyth.price_momentum` and `btc.pyth.price_volatility` are defined ahead of their processors): twenty-one blended spot, ninety-two per-venue spot (twenty-three each under `btc.coinbase.*`, `btc.kraken.*`, `btc.binance.*` and `btc.bybit.*`, ADR 0024 and the Binance/Bybit spot-sibling ADR 0028 — the Binance and Bybit siblings are rights-gated and carry a constant `quote_currency` field per ruling R6; the twenty-third per-venue spot identity is C3b's `tick_velocity`, ruling E51), twenty-six Binance/Bybit derivatives-lane identities (Bybit fourteen, Binance twelve after ruling E62 (2026-09-04) deleted `perp_cvd`/`liquidation_pressure`: five/three B2 families, two C2 perp momentum/volatility identities and seven `perp_`-prefixed technical-analysis identities, ADR 0026 and rulings E49/E51/E62), forty-one venue-scoped prediction identities (twenty Polymarket, twenty-one Kalshi — the extra one is the index-armed `window_streak`; C3b, ruling E51, added `rsi`, `roc`, `zscore`, `tick_velocity` and `expected_value` to each prediction venue plus that streak), seven oracle reference identities (`btc.chainlink.*` (3), `btc.cfbrti.*` (1) and the refuse-closed `btc.pyth.*` (3)), three `btc.cross.*` tier-3 identities (`perp_spot_basis`, `liquidation_vs_spot_response`, `derivatives_regime`; C1, ruling E52), and three `btc.multi.*` tier-3 identities (`pair_cost_spread`, `prediction_dislocation`, and the refuse-closed `oracle_dispersion`; C1, ruling E57). The C3a technical-analysis pack (ruling E51) accounts for forty-nine of these: `atr`, `rsi`, `roc`, `ema_cross`, `macd`, `bollinger` and `zscore` on each of the five spot scopes (blended plus the four venues) and, under the `perp_` prefix, on each of the two perpetual mark scopes.

These files specify intended boundaries. Definition lifecycle and implementation status are tracked separately; an experimental contract does not claim production integration or deployment.
